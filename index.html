Prototype · concept solution

From webshop to guided collector journey.

This clickable prototype translates the research into a clearer online customer journey for J Motorsport Trading. The concept helps international customers find suitable products faster, understand authenticity more clearly, and feel more confident during the buying process.

The branding is now aligned more closely with the current J Motorsport Trading look and feel: clean, premium, black/white/orange, and focused on the real company identity instead of a generic “JMM” label.

J Motorsport Trading

Premium race-used Formula 1 memorabilia

A more guided, more trustworthy and more brand-consistent experience for customers who want to browse, compare and buy authentic motorsport items.

Linked to the research question

What the prototype is trying to solve

01 · ORIENTATION

Faster entry into the webshop

Customers can start from budget, product type or driver interest instead of browsing without guidance.

02 · CONFIDENCE

More certainty before purchase

Customers get product examples, clearer context and a prototype trust layer around authenticity and provenance.

03 · TRUST

Stronger authenticity communication

The final part shows how a digital authenticity page could support the physical COA without exposing confidential supplier information.
